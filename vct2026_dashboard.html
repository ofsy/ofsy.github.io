Agent Pick & Performance Metrics

Analyzing agent pick-rates reveals that the 2026 competitive landscape is heavily dominated by flexible controllers and specialized duelists.

Agent Meta Observations: The custom agent Waylay has carved a significant footprint, representing 38.9% overall pick rate. With a combat performance average rating of 0.94 Rating and a high average combat score of 200.3, it has established itself as an essential meta pick.

Economy Round Win Rates

Economics governs Valorant matches. Round outcomes depend heavily on loadout purchases. Here we analyze round conversions based on team loadout value tiers.

Economy Win Rates:
  • • Full buy (20k+): Stands at a comfortable ~52% win rate.
  • • Force-buy (5-10k): Conversion rates hover around ~45%.
  • • Eco rounds (0-5k): Win rates collapse to ~22%, marking these rounds as highly defensive or sacrificial.
