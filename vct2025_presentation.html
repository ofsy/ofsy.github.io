Map Win Rate as a Core Metric

Match-level records can mask margins. A team winning 2-0 is demonstrating map pool dominance, whereas teams winning 2-1 are dropping maps to lower seeds.

  • G2 Esports holds the highest map-level win-rate at 69.00% (69 wins, 31 losses).
  • Paper Rex is 2nd in map win rate (65.48%) but holds a higher match win rate (73.33%).
  • This divergence suggests that G2 is extremely dominant on their map selections, even when series run close.

💰 The Thrifty Paradox: Save vs. Force Tiers

Buy round win conversions

Loadout values govern win rates. However, saving completely for full guns can be more valuable than forced low-budget buys.

*Takeaway:* Force-buying cheap intermediate gear on Semi-Eco (5-10k) is highly inefficient, yielding a low **22.06% win rate**. A full save Eco (0-5k) using Sheriffs/Marshals achieves a **36.45% win rate** (1.6x higher).

💡 Executive Strategic Recommendations

For General Managers, Head Coaches, and analysts looking to design competitive championship-grade Valorant compositions in future stages.

1 Double smokers

Enforce Omen + Viper layouts on Bind, Ascent, and Lotus. The high smoke output and retake flexibility yield a **54.50% win rate**.

2 Save Discipline

Enforce strict saves on Eco (0-5k) instead of force-buying cheap gear. Full saves convert at **36.45% WR**, nearly double the force-buy rate.

3 Map Advantage & Meta

Align drafts with map side advantage and meta agent picks. Attacker-sided maps like Abyss (14.5% gap) require high-pick initiator/controller setups (Sova 94.1%, Astra 68.6%), while defender-sided maps like Ascent (9.2% gap) demand anchoring meta controllers like Omen (97.6% pick rate) to secure rounds.
